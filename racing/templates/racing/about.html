Canadian cross country race results typically end up in PDFs from meet management programs like HY-TEK, Easy Meet Manager, and others.

These PDFs are hard to read and isolated from one another. This site aims to make these results more accessible:

  • Each race has individual and team results
  • Data is linked together across races (runners, teams, etc.)
  • Results are presented consistently in HTML (not PDFs!)

For more information, see the introductory blog post.
